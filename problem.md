Problem 13:
Given n(n≤10^3) points with integer coordinates such that no two coincide and no three are collinear (n is even). 
Task: Select 2 of the n points such that the line through them splits the remaining points into two equal halves.
Input:
First line: n2≤n≤103
Line i of the n following lines: xi,yixi,yi106 — coordinates of point i.
Output: 
Two integers — the indices of the two selected points.
Example:
INPUT
6
3 5
1 3
3 1
6 1
8 3
6 5
OUTPUT
2 5
